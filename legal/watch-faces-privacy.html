Privacy Policy for MMM Concept Watch Faces

Last updated: Jan 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the watch face applications developed by MMM Concept for Wear OS devices. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our watch faces are designed to be functional and decorative.

No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers).

Sensors and Health Data: If the watch face displays heart rate, step counts, or other fitness data, this information is processed locally on your device only. We do not transmit this data to any external servers.

Location Data: If the watch face displays weather information, it may use your device's location to provide local weather updates. This data is not stored or shared by us.

3. Third-Party Services

Our watch faces do not use third-party analytics or advertising frameworks that collect user data.

4. Permissions

The watch face may request permissions to access:

  • Body Sensors: To display heart rate.
  • Complications: To display data from other apps (e.g., battery level, weather).

These permissions are used strictly for the functionality of the watch face.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
