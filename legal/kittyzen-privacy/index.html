Privacy Policy for Kittyzen: Pet Arcade

Last updated: Nov 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Kittyzen: Pet Arcade, a game for Wear OS watches and its companion app for Android phones. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

Who we are: The game is published by Marcin Morawski, trading as MMM Concept, Grodzisk Mazowiecki, Poland ("we"). Contact: contact@mmmconcept.com. The app does not send your game data to us, so we are the data controller only for the limited data described in section 6 (support messages).

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our game is designed to be fun and simple.

No Personal Data: The app does not collect, store, or send to us any personal information (such as names or phone numbers). The app does not require an account, and we do not operate any servers that receive data from the app.

Data Stored on Your Device: The game saves your progress (coins, level, experience, your cat's state, unlocked items, scores, and settings) locally on your watch. This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data, unless a backup described below restores it.

Sync Between Watch and Phone: If you use both the watch game and the phone companion app, your cat's state is sent from the watch to the phone and shown in the companion app. This uses the Google Wearable Data Layer, which is part of Google Play services. We do not receive or store this data.

Purchase Status Between Phone and Watch: If you buy the Premium upgrade in the companion app, the phone tells the watch that Premium is bought, using the same Google Wearable Data Layer, so that the watch can unlock Premium at once. This is a single yes/no value with a timestamp. The watch also keeps the last value you bought or were told about on the device, so that Premium keeps working offline. We do not receive or store this data.

Progress Backup on Your Phone: If you have the companion app, a backup copy of your game progress (the same data as above, without your purchase status) is kept on your phone. If you reinstall the game on the watch, the watch asks your phone for this copy and restores your progress. Without the companion app, no such backup exists.

Optional Backup in Your Google Account: In the companion app you can choose to sign in to Google Play Games. If you do, the backup copy of your progress is also stored as a saved game in your own Google account (Google Play Games Services), so it can survive the loss or reset of your phone. This is optional and happens only after you sign in; if you do not sign in, nothing is stored in your Google account by the app. We do not receive this data and cannot see it. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy.

System Backup: Your device's own backup feature (Android backup to your Google account, where your device supports and has enabled it) may also include the app's saved progress. This is controlled by your device settings and handled by Google, not by us.

3. Purchases

The game offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (the Premium upgrade) through Google Play Billing. You can buy it on the watch or in the companion app on your phone; it is the same purchase, tied to your Google account. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. In Google Play Console we may see limited order information (such as order ID, product, country and refund status), and your device receives confirmation from Google Play so that the purchased features can be unlocked. If you buy on the phone, the phone passes the purchase status to the watch as described in section 2. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Our game does not use third-party analytics or advertising frameworks that collect user data. The only third-party services involved are those provided by Google as part of the Android and Wear OS platform: Google Play (distribution and billing, on the watch and in the companion app), Google Play services (Wearable Data Layer), and, only if you sign in to it in the companion app, Google Play Games Services (saved game backup). Google acts as a separate controller for its own services and may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area under its own terms and safeguards, described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not transfer any data ourselves.

5. Permissions

The app may request or use the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To remind you to feed and play with your cat. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Vibration: To give haptic feedback in the game. This does not access any data.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, health sensors, or files. These permissions are used strictly for the functionality of the game.

6. Contacting Us

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message only to reply and to handle your request (legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing support, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or taking steps at your request, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep correspondence for up to 24 months after the last message. Our email is hosted by Google, which acts as our service provider. You do not have to contact us, but we cannot answer you otherwise.

7. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data, no such information is stored or shared by us.

8. Your Rights and Data Deletion

We operate no servers and hold no copy of your game data that we could delete on your behalf. You can delete your data yourself at any time:

  • On your devices: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app from your watch and your phone. Uninstalling the companion app removes the backup copy kept on your phone.
  • In your Google account: if you used the optional Google Play Games features, you can delete the saved game and your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app and through your Google Account settings. Any system backup of your device can be managed or deleted in your device and Google Account settings.

Regarding the data we do process (support correspondence), you have the right to access, rectify, erase or restrict the processing of your data, to object, and to data portability. Contact us at the address below to use these rights. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), https://uodo.gov.pl, or with the authority in your country of residence.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page together with a new "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will say so at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@mmmconcept.com
