Privacy Policy for MMM Concept Watch Faces

Last updated: Nov 15, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the watch face applications developed by MMM Concept for Wear OS devices. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

Who we are: The watch faces are published by Marcin Morawski, trading as MMM Concept, Grodzisk Mazowiecki, Poland ("we"). Contact: marcin.mariusz.morawski.dev@gmail.com. The watch faces do not send any data to us, so we are the data controller only for the limited data described in section 6 (support messages).

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our watch faces are designed to be functional and decorative.

No Personal Data: The watch faces do not collect, store, or send to us any personal information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers). They do not require an account, and we do not operate any servers that receive data from the watch faces.

Sensors and Health Data: If the watch face displays heart rate, step counts, or other fitness data, this information is read and shown on your device only. We do not receive it and do not transmit it to any external servers.

Weather and Other Data: The watch faces do not access your location and do not download weather data themselves. Information such as weather or battery level is shown through "complications", which are provided by other apps you choose on your watch. Those apps are responsible for their own data handling under their own privacy policies.

3. Purchases

Our watch faces are paid products sold through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. In Google Play Console we may see limited order information (such as order ID, product, country and refund status). Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Our watch faces do not use third-party analytics or advertising frameworks that collect user data. The only third-party services involved are those provided by Google as part of the Android and Wear OS platform (Google Play for distribution and billing). Google may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area under its own terms and safeguards, described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not transfer any data ourselves.

5. Permissions

The watch face may request permissions to access:

  • Body Sensors: To display heart rate.
  • Complications: To display data from other apps (e.g., battery level, weather).

These permissions are used strictly for the functionality of the watch face.

6. Contacting Us

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message only to reply and to handle your request (legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing support, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or taking steps at your request, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep correspondence for up to 24 months after the last message. Our email is hosted by Google, which acts as our service provider. You do not have to contact us, but we cannot answer you otherwise.

7. Children's Privacy

Our watch faces are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Your Rights

Regarding the data we process (support correspondence), you have the right to access, rectify, erase or restrict the processing of your data, to object, and to data portability. Contact us at the address below to use these rights. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), https://uodo.gov.pl, or with the authority in your country of residence.

Data shown on your watch stays on your device. You can remove it by uninstalling the watch face.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page together with a new "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will say so at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: marcin.mariusz.morawski.dev@gmail.com
