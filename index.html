Games and watch faces for Wear OS

MMM Concept is a small creative studio making games and watch faces for Wear OS smartwatches. Polished details, stable performance, your data stays yours.

Our apps

Kittyzen: Pet Arcade

Game for Wear OS

Care for a pixel cat right on your wrist and play quick arcade minigames to earn coins. Dress your cat up, level it up and collect badges. No ads, no accounts.

MMM Lunar Flow Animated

Watch face for Wear OS

The real phase of the Moon on your wrist, with twinkling stars and a softly pulsing nebula. It follows the lunar cycle on its own, with three data slots you can customise and an always-on mode.

Google Play

MMM Abyss Deep Sea Explorer

Watch face for Wear OS

A glowing deep-sea scene with a diver, jellyfish and neon coral on your wrist.

Google Play

MMM Emberling Dragon Pet

Watch face for Wear OS

A pixel fire dragon that grows with your daily steps. It hatches as an egg, becomes a serpent and finally a winged dragon once you reach your goal. Four customisable ring slots and an always-on mode.

Google Play

MMM Steppeak: Step Goal Hiker

Watch face for Wear OS

A pixel hiker climbs a mountain as you walk towards your daily step goal. Six scenes from the foot of the mountain to the summit flag, a gold progress arc, your steps against your goal and two customisable slots. Your steps stay on your watch.

Google Play

Pocket Tower Defense

Game for Wear OS

Tower defense on the round screen of your watch: build towers beside the path, upgrade or sell them, and hold off 15 waves of goblins, orcs and wolves, with a boss every fifth wave. Free version with 2 maps; a one-time purchase unlocks 5 more maps, Endless mode and elite towers. No ads.

Google Play
