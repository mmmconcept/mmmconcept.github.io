Privacy Policy for Pocket Tower Defense

Last updated: Nov 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Pocket Tower Defense, a game for Wear OS watches and its companion app for Android phones, developed by MMM Concept. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our game is designed to be fun and simple.

No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers). The app does not require an account, and we do not operate any servers that receive data from the app.

Data Stored on Your Device: The game saves your progress (best wave on each map, maps won, high score, enemies defeated, talents, achievements, and settings such as volume) locally on your watch. This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data, unless a backup described below restores it.

Sync Between Watch and Phone: If you use both the watch game and the phone companion app, your progress is sent from the watch to the phone and shown in the companion app. This uses the Google Wearable Data Layer, which is part of Google Play services. We do not receive or store this data.

Progress Backup on Your Phone: If you have the companion app, a backup copy of your game progress (the same data as above, without your purchase status) is kept on your phone. If you reinstall the game on the watch, the watch asks your phone for this copy and restores your progress. Without the companion app, no such backup exists. The phone also tells the watch whether the full game has been purchased.

Score Card: If you share your score from the companion app, the image is created on your phone and goes only where you choose to share it, through the Android share menu.

System Backup: Your device's own backup feature (Android backup to your Google account, where your device supports and has enabled it) may also include the app's saved progress. This is controlled by your device settings and handled by Google, not by us.

3. Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (the full game) through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase has been made, in order to unlock the purchased features. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Our game does not show ads and does not use third-party analytics or advertising frameworks that collect user data. The only third-party services involved are those provided by Google as part of the Android and Wear OS platform: Google Play (distribution and billing) and Google Play services (Wearable Data Layer).

5. Permissions

The app may request or use the following permissions:

  • Vibration: To give haptic feedback in the game. This does not access any data.
  • Wake lock: To keep the game running correctly in the watch's ambient (dimmed) mode. This does not access any data.
  • Network access: Only as needed by Google Play (billing) and the Wearable Data Layer. The app does not send your data to our servers.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, health sensors, or files. These permissions are used strictly for the functionality of the game.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data, no such information from children is stored or shared by us.

7. Data Deletion

We operate no servers and hold no copy of your data that we could delete on your behalf. You can delete your data yourself at any time:

  • On your devices: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app from your watch and your phone. Uninstalling the companion app removes the backup copy kept on your phone.
  • In your Google account: any system backup of your device can be managed or deleted in your device and Google Account settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
